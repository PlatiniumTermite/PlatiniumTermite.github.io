Use case

Every device you own, finally sharing like they should.

AirDrop, Quick Share, Nearby Share — each ecosystem built sharing that only works inside its own walls. Flow is the layer underneath: one way to move anything between a MacBook, a Windows PC, an Android phone and an iPad.

The mixed-device reality Flow is for

MacBook + Android

AirDrop doesn't see the phone; Quick Share doesn't see the Mac. Flow sees both — share from Android's share sheet straight to the Mac's Downloads.

Windows PC + iPad

The iPad joins through Safari — scan the PC's QR invitation and files move both ways with approval, no install on iOS.

Work PC + personal Mac

Two computers on one desk act like one machine — shared clipboard, screen view, even keyboard/mouse link with approval.

Everyone in the house

Friend sessions give visitors an isolated space — they send you files without touching your devices' personal space or clipboard.

How it fits together

Install on the devices you own

Windows installer, Mac DMG, Android APK — iPhones and iPads join via Safari, no install.

They find each other on Wi-Fi

Automatic mDNS discovery — devices appear by name. No router? A phone hotspot is a perfectly good network.

Everything flows

Files, folders, text, links, clipboard, screens — any direction, approval-gated, resumable.

One household, one system.

Free · no accounts · nothing leaves your network.
