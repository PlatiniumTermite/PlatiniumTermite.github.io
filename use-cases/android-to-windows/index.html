Use case

Android phone ↔ Windows PC, wirelessly.

Photos, documents, links and clipboard moving between your phone and PC like they belong to the same system — no cables, no cloud drives, no emailing yourself.

Set it up once

Install Flow on both

The Windows installer on the PC and the APK on Android. Both join the same Wi-Fi — the phone's hotspot works too.

Pair with approval

The phone appears in the PC's Nearby list (and vice versa). Tap Connect on one, Allow on the other — paired until you remove it.

Share from anywhere on Android

In Photos, Files or Chrome: Share → Flow → pick the PC. The PC can also push files, text and links back to the phone — both directions work.

What you can do

Photos & videos to PC

Shoot on the phone, share to the PC — lands in Downloads with the original quality, full folders supported.

Files back to the phone

Documents, APKs, music — send from the PC's Flow window; the phone approves and saves them.

Links & text

Found something on the phone, need it on the big screen — send it straight to the PC's inbox.

Clipboard handoff

Copy a password or address on the PC and paste it on the phone — Android captures clipboard while the app is open or via the notification tap.

Honest Android notes

  • Background clipboard is OS-limited. Android blocks clipboard reads for apps that aren't in the foreground — that's true for every third-party app. Flow's notification-tap path is the closest legal capture.
  • Keep Flow alive for continuous sharing — Android battery savers can suspend it; exempt it in battery settings if transfers seem to pause.
  • Play Protect warns once since the APK is outside the Play Store: More details → Install anyway.
