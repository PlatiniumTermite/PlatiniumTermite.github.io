How it works

Your devices. One connected experience.

Flow makes your computers and phone aware of each other on your own network — then moves files, clipboard and screens between them directly, device to device.

1 · Discovery — devices find each other

When Flow runs on two devices on the same Wi-Fi, each announces itself over mDNS — the same Bonjour technology Apple uses for AirDrop discovery. Your other computer simply appears in the Nearby list, named by its device label. No accounts, no QR codes required for your own machines.

Flow lives in the Mac menu bar and Windows system tray, so discovery keeps working even when no window is open — like AirDrop, being "there" is the default state.

2 · Pairing — approval, always

Nothing connects silently. When one device taps Connect, the other shows a pairing request with the requester's name; only Allow creates the link. Friend sessions work the same way through invitation links or QR codes — and they stay isolated from your personal space and clipboard.

3 · Transfers — peer to peer, not through a server

Once paired, Flow opens a WebRTC connection between the devices and sends data over an encrypted channel directly — files, folders, text and links never touch a third-party server. Transfers show live progress, handle filename collisions in Downloads, resume from the last byte after a disconnect, and can go to several recipients independently.

Everything that moves

Files & folders

Any size, whole folders, with checksums, resume and send-to-many. From the share sheet on Android, or Send To / Open With on desktops.

Clipboard

Copy on one device, paste on another — text and images between desktops, like Universal Clipboard. A device joining later inherits the current clipboard.

Screen sharing

View another computer's screen with live metrics — only after the owner accepts. Stopping is one click and kills the capture.

Extended display

Experimental: a Mac can extend its desktop onto a connected computer over Wi-Fi, and the receiver's own keyboard and mouse can drive it.

Keyboard & mouse link

Control a connected computer's input in either direction — approval-gated, heartbeat-supervised, one click or Escape to release.

Queued & resumable

Sends to a device that's still connecting queue up and deliver when it arrives. Reconnects pick up where they left off.

When devices aren't on the same Wi-Fi

AirDrop sidesteps this with Apple's private radios — no third-party app can use those. Flow's nearest equivalents:

  • Phone hotspot — one device hosts, both join; it's a normal LAN, so discovery works identically. Fastest option.
  • Invitation links / QR — works wherever the connection address is reachable.
  • Self-hosted relay — advanced users can point Flow at their own wss:// signaling endpoint for internet-wide pairing.
